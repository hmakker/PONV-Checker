PONV risk factors

Risk factors are not considered for cesarean delivery. Every cesarean needs two classes.

Prophylaxis given

Count only agents given from 4 hours before preop start to PACU start. Routes that count: IV, IM, oral, sublingual, rectal, intraosseous, enteric tube; scopolamine also topical or transdermal.

Rules this checker applies

Included: age 18 or older under general anesthesia with at least one risk factor, or any cesarean delivery regardless of age or technique. Excluded: under 18 (except cesarean), ASA 5 or 6, organ procurement, direct ICU transfer, bronchoscopy only, diagnostic imaging only, ECT, endoscopy or ERCP only, intubation only, labor epidural, TEE or cardioversion, and surgery of 30 minutes or less (not applied to cesarean).

  • 1 to 2 risk factors: at least 2 antiemetics from different classes.
  • 3 or more risk factors: at least 3 antiemetics from different classes.
  • Cesarean: at least 2 from different classes. Recommended classes are 5-HT3 antagonists, glucocorticoids and antidopaminergics.
  • Propofol infusion counts as one option. A 2025 committee review voted to add midazolam; this checker does not count it until your MPOG build does.

Class assignment of individual drugs follows the class names in the specifications. Droperidol, haloperidol, metoclopramide and amisulpride are grouped together as antidopaminergics, which is the conservative reading; confirm against your local MPOG build if it splits them.

Based on the ASPIRE PONV-05 (published 01/2022, revised 09/2024) specification. This is a bedside aid, not the MPOG abstraction itself, and it does not give dosing advice.
